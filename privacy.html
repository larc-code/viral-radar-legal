Viral Radar Privacy Policy

Viral Radar is a personal local application.

When authorized, TikTok account data, video metadata, and authorization tokens are stored only on the user's own computer.

Viral Radar does not sell, share, or transmit this data to third parties.

The user may revoke TikTok authorization in TikTok settings and can delete the app's local data at any time.
